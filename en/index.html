Why Spain

Strong reasons to lease in Spain

Strong sales potential

In the Spanish zone, shows and events become buying pauses in front of storefronts. Lease here and you sell where the crowd stops after the performance — not where it rushes past. A unit near the plaza rides the evening’s own rhythm.

Bring in many customers

The pedestrian path circles the plaza and brings visitors past the shops more than once. Families and guests who came for photos and shows become a natural audience for your brand inside the destination — without hunting for them outside.

Plant your visual identity in the place — and introduce yourself to customers

Your frontage is part of the scene people photograph: the arch, the fountain, the night lighting. Your visual presence lodges in the visitor’s memory with the plaza shot — they recognise you before they enter and remember you after they leave.

Share your brand’s culture with shoppers and with families

The audience is mixed: gift hunters, diners, and families strolling with children. Your offer is shown in an open entertainment setting — reaching buyers and families in the same moment.

Market your brand

Leasing in Spain places your brand inside a destination people visit for the experience — not a quick pass-through. Every visit, every photo at the arch, every evening around the fountain retells your name in the season’s own context.

01 — The destination

What is the destination

Boulevard World has operated since 2022 and returns this year for the fifth time within the seventh edition of Riyadh Season. It is not a sealed mall: open streets, a lake at the centre, and pavilions built as cities.

Located in Hittin, north Riyadh, next to Boulevard City. Visitors enter with a ticket, then move between Saudi Arabia, Japan, France, Spain and more in a single night. Leasing here is not about a quiet daytime street shop — it is about an evening of walking, food, and photos.

The four figures above come from the destination’s official site. Always check tickets and timings on Webook — they change by day and show.

02 — Spain

The Spanish zone is not décor. It is a night-time sales path.

«Discover the atmosphere of Spain among live shows and distinctive architecture, with famous dishes, popular markets, and traditional products.»
Official description on blvdworld.sa
«Spain’s zone highlights architecture that takes visitors back to ancient eras as they move through squares crowded with passers-by and cafés that hold those who stroll past.»
Al-Rai newspaper, 16 December 2022 — The matador dances flamenco at Boulevard World

The guest came to stroll — not to rush

An entry ticket turns the visit into an evening out. Anyone who pays to enter stays among restaurants, photos, and shows — selling time that is nothing like a midday mall.

The plaza works for the storefront

The design is circular. A unit does not vanish behind a long corridor. The visitor photographing the gate and fountain is already standing in front of the shops.

The new neighbour doubles the traffic

Portugal opens this season right beside Spain. Two cultural brands side by side — Lisbon and Madrid — on one pedestrian path.

Unit types that cover more than one model

A kiosk for a lighter brand, a retail shop for display, and an F&B unit for seating. One client can start with a kiosk or lock in a restaurant — depending on how they operate.
